# Stage 2: decoding calibration and contextual donors

## Protocol

Seed 42 only, frozen archived checkpoints, local CPU, four workers sharing eight
cores. Ladder Reward-State and moving transfer use eight independent episodes in
each of fit, held-out diagnostics, lambda selection and confirmation. Full-State
and both XOR agents retain two episodes per split. Episodes have 1,024 decisions.
The exact filters condition on observations and executed actions, not rewards.
Fit and diagnostic trajectories use seeds 42 and 44; causal subspace fitting uses
independent 42/43/44 train/selection/test pairs. Closed-loop tuning and confirmation
reuse the archived disjoint seeds. These trajectory seeds are not agent seeds.
No paid compute, training or changes to earlier study results.

Stage-2 gradient writes use 60 optimizer steps instead of 20. Historical Eq. (4)
and norm-null strengths remain fixed at the archive values; candidates use the
historical lambda grids and selection rules. XOR has no preserved factor, so its
Jacobian-null direction equals Eq. (4); differing scores only reflect retuning.
Ladder `eq4_joint` duplicates `eq4`; ladder `map` duplicates intact and is not a
Bayes-controller reference. The moving map is an exact-filter reference controller,
not an optimality bound. Raw reports and per-method timings are in
[`stage2_tables.md`](stage2_tables.md), generated by `stage2_report.py`.

## Why the earlier gradient pilots exactly equaled intact

The original result files and a fresh reproduction both select lambda zero for
ladder band/MLP and v2 band. Lambda zero produces exactly zero applied shift.
Ladder tuning scores were identical across the entire nonzero grid; v2 band was
tied with zero through lambda 1.25 and worse at larger strengths. The ladder
mirror test fixes lambda one, but its small shifts also left argmax actions
unchanged in those episodes.

This is not evidence that gradient writes were skipped. The reachable runner
branch calls `gradient_write`, then adds `strength * delta` at site 3 in
`base.forward`. The same final layer norm and policy head are used by
`transfer.logits` for the gradient objective and by `base.forward` for rollout.
To try to disprove a decoder-only explanation, fixed held-out rows from the
original two-episode fits were optimized for 20, 60 and 200 steps. Targets were
constructed exactly as in the ladder rho-flip or XOR rollout, not initialized at
an already-satisfied target. Results are saved in `results/diagnosis_*.json` in
both study folders; these diagnostic samples are the first 128 post-warmup rows,
not independent trajectory replicates.

| Decoder/test | Initial MSE | MSE at 20 steps | MSE at 200 steps | Shift norm at 20 | Policy TV at 20 | Policy TV at 200 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Band / ladder | .19152 | .007751 | 2.25e-8 | .451 | .0000414 | .0000302 |
| MLP / ladder | .20373 | .004751 | .0000113 | 2.065 | .000810 | .000288 |
| Band / v2 | .17045 | .005587 | .00000576 | 2.695 | .01054 | .00639 |

Thus gradients are nonzero, targets initially differ substantially, and 20 steps
already remove most target error. More steps nearly solve the probe objective
without producing larger policy effects. The confirmed immediate causes are
lambda-zero selection and tiny/subthreshold policy changes; the broader
interpretation is that probe-target optimization can exploit directions the
policy barely uses. No wiring bug was found and no speculative optimizer fix was
applied. Increasing to 60 steps is a controlled sensitivity check, not a claim
that the decoding limitation is fixed.

## Policy-implied calibration

The tables report held-out `KL(agent || map(Bayes))` and total variation at the
same temperature 1.5, training KL, and a training-mean-policy baseline. The inverse
uses the same fitted map, simplex constraints, uniform initialization, and 100
optimization steps as stage 1. Per-episode KL/TV are saved; no timestep-based
confidence intervals are used. Inverse–Bayes probability, rho and signed-delta
MAEs and inverse-policy KL are reported separately.

Even a well-calibrated map is not necessarily injective; a poorly calibrated map
can instead make the inverse compensate for forward-model misspecification.
Accordingly neither a large nor small inverse–Bayes gap alone establishes whether
the policy tracks the true latent state. Full-State is a positive control, not a
guarantee that this affine forward model is correctly specified.

## Contextual donor implementation

Four new methods cross exact-filter versus policy-implied bank labels with
subspace versus full-block-4 patching:

- `context_exact`, `context_policy`: patch the target readout row-space.
- `context_exact_full`, `context_policy_full`: replace the entire latest block-4
  activation at lambda one; other strengths interpolate/extrapolate.

The target subspace is the numerical row-space of the fitted affine belief
readout (the Eq. (4)-style readout-complement option), with `rcond=1e-6`. Moving
uses only factor 2; XOR uses both factors; ladder uses the full three-state
readout. The basis and complement are shared by the full/subspace variants.
Bank rows are every fourth post-warmup fit activation. Exact variants label
both bank and current history with the exact decision-time filter; policy
variants use the calibrated inverse on both. Exact-filter methods are
filter-assisted controllers, not demonstrations of belief decoding from h.

A donor must have max probability-coordinate error at most .15 on the target
factor and at most .15 on the non-target factor. Among qualifying donors choose
the nearest activation in the orthogonal complement, scaled by training
activation standard deviations, plus squared non-target belief mismatch. If
there is no qualifying donor, abstain. No test rows enter the bank or thresholds.
Coverage is saved per rollout (eligibility before lambda scaling, not the fraction
of nonzero writes). Full replacements are real bank activations at lambda one;
subspace hybrids and lambda-scaled shifts are not guaranteed on-manifold, and
activation-space closeness does not guarantee matching latent histories.

## Resumable execution

One local invocation runs all methods for a test and agent seed:

```sh
uv run --group jax python -m experiments.reward_factors_causal_decoding_2026_11.run \
  --test moving --seed 42 --method all --stage2 --workers 4
```

`--stage2` implies pilot mode with the eight-episode overrides above. For full
budgets omit `--stage2`; for stage-1 budgets use `--pilot`. The same entry point
supports `ladder`, `ladder_full`, `moving`, `xor_eps02`, and `xor_v2`. Use the
ladder wrapper if desired. Results are atomic per-method JSON replacements;
completed compatible outputs print `SKIP`, while malformed/incompatible files
raise rather than silently overwrite. Interrupted `.partial` files are ignored
and recomputed. Resume is per method, not from inside an interrupted lambda sweep.
Use separate output prefixes/budgets for revisions; resume does not certify that
an old result was produced by identical source code. Stage-1 files remain intact.

For an already-provisioned GPU box, use the installed `jax-cuda` group instead
of `jax` if supported by its image; no boxes were provisioned or GPU runs tested.

Regenerate all tables and the SHA256 manifest after the pilot completes:

```sh
uv run --group jax python -m experiments.reward_factors_causal_decoding_2026_11.stage2_report
```

## Updated measurements and interpretation

- Ladder Eq. (4): .1282 occupancy / .0105 mirror actions changed. DAS: .1338 /
  .1095. Exact-context full donor: .1263 / .0193. The donor's small occupancy
  advantage is accompanied by greater spillover and is not evidence of superiority.
  Policy-implied full donor reaches only .4010 occupancy. Readout-subspace donors
  select lambda zero, even with complete exact-label bank support.
- Moving joint Eq. (4): 1.0144 reward, .741 gap closed, .3353 factor-1 reward,
  .0747 factor-1 actions changed. Policy-implied full donor: 1.0347, .822, .3866,
  .0641 respectively, with .688 eligible-donor coverage. This is the most promising
  contextual variant in this pilot, but .0203 reward difference on eight episodes
  from one agent seed is not an established gain. Policy-implied gradient also
  reaches 1.0188 with .0258 factor-1 action changes.
- v2: DAS remains .7021 versus joint Eq. (4) .6924; exact-context full donor is
  .6753. eps02: no contextual variant exceeds factored Eq. (4) .6406. Its .7104
  "null" result is only a differently tuned Eq. (4) direction, not a new method.
- The larger ladder fits still leave band/MLP occupancy exactly at intact .4480.
  Band selects lambda zero, MLP selects .25; their mirror action-change rates are
  only .00012. At v2 band still selects zero with 60 steps. The diagnosis therefore
  survives both more optimization and, for ladder, more fit/evaluation episodes.

Policy-map held-out KL/TV are .10180/.08767 for Reward-State, .07390/.07335 for
Full-State, .29738/.25054 for moving, .35028/.27706 for eps02 XOR, and
.20055/.18699 for v2 XOR. All beat the constant mean-policy baseline, but they
are not negligible errors. Inverse probability MAE is .1788 for Reward-State
versus .1060 for Full-State; the corresponding signed-delta errors are .3468 and
.1149. The positive control has smaller gaps but does not make the map exact.
Inverse-policy KL is much smaller than map(Bayes) KL (e.g. .00606 versus .10180
on Reward-State), demonstrating that the fitted inverse can compensate for
forward-map mismatch. These measurements do not establish failure to track Bayes
belief. A more expressive, validated forward map and an identifiability analysis
are needed before making that claim.

The complete run allocated 2.546 core-hours across final jobs, with policy-gradient
jobs taking 304–977 seconds versus 8–51 seconds for contextual donors. Timing
includes restore, fits, diagnostics and lambda selection, not only rollout. No
three-seed or full-budget runs were performed. Relative to the archived twin
findings, the same caveat persists: replay/subspace fidelity or a v2 reward lead
does not ensure selective steering, and eps02 remains the harder test.
